Word Cards

Sight-word flashcards for young readers. Your child reads each word out loud, the phone listens, and the cards adapt to the words they find hard. No ads, no accounts, no internet needed.

How it works

🎤

Hands-free

Pick a color and just read. Speech recognition runs on the phone, so nothing is sent anywhere.

🔁

Spaced repetition

Missed words come back a few cards later. Words they already know show up less often.

🦊

Ember the fox

Ember cheers when a word is read right. Each reader can pick their own fox: Ember, Snowy, Pixel, Manga or Origami.

🤷

Made for non-readers

The buttons are pictures. Tap 🤷 to hear the word. Every word is pre-recorded, so it plays right away.

👧👦

Several readers

Each child gets their own progress, fox and listening time.

🔒

Kid lock

Pins the app with a grown-up PIN, so the child can't wander off into other apps.

Grading from how they read

When the child…The card counts as
reads it within ~2.5 s on the first tryEasy
reads it within ~6 s, at most one wrong tryGood
gets there eventuallyHard
doesn't say it in time, or taps 🤷Again: the word is read aloud

Install

  1. On the Android phone, tap Download for Android.
  2. Open the downloaded word-cards.apk. If Android asks, allow your browser to install apps.
  3. To update, install the newer APK from the releases page over the old one. Progress is kept.
